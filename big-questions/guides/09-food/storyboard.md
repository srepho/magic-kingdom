# Guide 09 — Where does food come from?

**Built** 14 November 2026, without a review stop. Decisions made along the way are listed
at the end. Fact checks are in `notes.md`.

## The guide in one line

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Single idea | Food starts as a plant or an animal on a farm and travels to reach the plate |
| Her action | She slides a food off the plate and back along the road |
| What she sees | The food turns into what it really was at each stop: shop, truck, the place it was made, and the farm |
| Misconception to avoid | That food starts at the shop. That bread, chips or milk are "made" from nothing |

**Taught terms (three):** **farm**, **crop**, **factory**. "Mill", "bakery", "orchard" and
"packing shed" are used as ordinary words in captions.

## Opening — breakfast

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| The insight | This is my food. Where was it before? |
| What she does | Taps the toast, the milk, the egg or the apple |
| What she sees | A bite goes (or the milk level drops) with crumbs; after three bites it is gone, then a fresh one arrives |
| What she can vary | Which food, how many bites |
| Misconception to avoid | None introduced. This scene only raises the question |
| How we know it works | She pokes the food within a few seconds, unprompted |

Caption after three bites: *"Yum! Where was your breakfast before it got to your plate?"* (10 words).
The pretend-glasses button then wiggles to lead to the toy.

## Centrepiece — the journey

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| The insight | Each food had a journey. Followed backwards, every journey ends at a plant or an animal on a farm |
| What she does | Drags the food along the road, or anywhere on the road. Taps a building to send the food there. Taps another food on the tray |
| What she sees | The food in a big bubble, changing to what it is at each stop. The stop it is at stands taller; the rest fade a little. Let go and it settles on the nearest stop |
| What she can vary | Direction and speed (it scrubs both ways). Which of five foods. Where she stops |
| Misconception to avoid | That the change is magic: each caption says what was done at that stop. That food begins at the shop |
| How we know it works | She slides one food to the farm, then picks another food to see where that one ends up |

The five journeys (farm first):

| Food | Plant or animal | Stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toast | plant (wheat) | farm → mill (flour) → bakery (loaf) → truck → shop → home |
| Milk | animal (cow) | farm → factory (bottled) → cold truck → shop → home |
| Boiled egg | animal (hen) | farm → packing shed (carton) → truck → shop → home |
| Apple | plant (tree) | farm → packing shed (box) → truck → shop → home |
| Hot chips | plant (potato) | farm → factory (cut, frozen) → freezer truck → shop → home |

One caption per stop, 15 words or fewer (checked by a test). The farm captions:

- *"Bread starts as wheat, a **crop** growing on a **farm**."*
- *"Milk comes from cows. A farmer milks them every day on a **farm**."*
- *"Eggs are laid by hens. Hens live on a **farm**."*
- *"Apples grow on trees. A farm full of fruit trees is an orchard."*
- *"Chips start as potatoes, a **crop** that grows under the ground!"*

Until she first touches it, the food wiggles and three small arrows point back down the road.

## Moment — the farm

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| The insight | Plants and animals on a farm each give something we eat |
| What she does | Pokes the wheat, the hen, the cow, the apple tree or the potato plant |
| What she sees | Wheat shakes out grain; the hen hops and lays an egg; the cow's bucket fills; an apple drops and bounces; the potato plant lifts out of the soil to show potatoes underneath. A thought bubble shows the food each becomes |
| What she can vary | Which one, how often. Eggs and apples pile up, the bucket fills in three goes |
| Misconception to avoid | That potatoes grow on a bush like apples: the soil hides them until she pulls |
| How we know it works | She pokes the potato plant a second time to see it again |

One caption per thing, shown when poked. Nothing here moves on its own except a small
wiggle on anything she has not yet tried.

## Moment — the sandwich

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| The insight | One meal can come from several plants and animals |
| What she does | Taps a layer of the sandwich |
| What she sees | The layer slides out and the plant or animal it started as appears beside it. Tap again and it slides back |
| What she can vary | Any layer, in any order, in or out |
| Misconception to avoid | That cheese is its own thing: it is tied to the cow |
| How we know it works | She opens more than one layer |

Layers: bread (wheat), lettuce (lettuce plant), tomato (tomato plant), cheese (cow), bread (wheat).

## Try it

Plate detectives: pick three things at a meal, guess plant or animal, find the label in the
kitchen, tell the story backwards. For the windowsill: a carrot top in a saucer of water.

## Wonder more

- What did you eat today that grew under the ground?
- How many people helped your toast get to you?
- Is there any food that is not from a plant or an animal?

## Decisions made without asking

1. **Five foods: toast, milk, egg, apple, hot chips.** Two animals, three plants; chips are
   the surprise.
2. **No meat in the toy.** The grown-up notes give one calm sentence for when she asks.
3. **The food scrubs along one straight road** and settles on the nearest stop when released,
   so every resting state has a clear caption.
4. **A large bubble shows the food's current form**, because the token on the road is small.
5. **Stops are per food**, so bread gets a mill and a bakery while the others get one making place.
6. **"A plant or an animal" is never stated as "every food"** on screen. Fungi, salt and water
   are handled in the notes and by the third wonder question.
7. **Taught terms are farm, crop and factory.** "Plant" and "animal" are treated as words she knows.
8. **The farm scene puts everything on one farm**, logged as a simplification.
9. **Place to visit: Calmsley Hill City Farm**, with a note to check its website.
10. **No brand names** anywhere; the chip bag and bottle are plain.
11. **The cast animals from guide 2 are not used.** The cow and hen here are farm animals, not characters.
