## Simplifications logged (also on the page)

- "Plant or animal" covers nearly all food, not all: fungi, salt, water.
- One farm, one truck: real chains have silos, tankers, warehouses and several trucks.
- Milk goes cow → factory in the toy; the farm vat and the tanker are skipped.
- Apples go straight to the shop; many are cold-stored for months.
- Toasting, boiling and heating at home are left out.
- The farm scene puts five kinds of farming on one farm.
- The egg caption says hens "live on a farm"; housing systems (cage, barn, free range) are not shown.
- The milk caption says "a farmer milks them"; in practice machines do the milking.
- Meat is not in the toy. The notes give one neutral sentence.

## Could not verify

- No single Australian source walks wheat from paddock to loaf; milling and baking rest on general knowledge.
- The Australian Eggs and APAL pages were found by search but not opened, so their exact wording is unchecked.
- I did not find an Australian source for the frozen-chip process.
- Calmsley Hill's current prices and hours.

## Kept out on purpose

- Brand names and logos.
- Any view on diet, farming methods or animal welfare.
- Fishing, imports and seasons, beyond one line in the follow-up questions.
